SchemaProof — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · Applies to the SchemaProof browser extension for Microsoft Edge and Mozilla Firefox

SchemaProof reads the web page you are on inside your own browser and keeps the result on your device. It does not collect analytics, does not build a profile, and never sends page content, URLs, or browsing history anywhere.

1. What the extension processes

DataWhere it staysWhy
The HTML of the page you scanYour browser onlyTo find and check structured data (JSON-LD, microdata, RDFa). It is never uploaded.
Pages you add to a site auditYour browser onlyEach page is fetched once, without cookies, directly from your browser to that website.
Scan results, reportsYour device (downloads / extension storage)So you can open, keep or delete them yourself.
Licence keyYour device, and Gumroad (only when you press “Verify key”)To confirm a Pro purchase.
Settings (e.g. licence check time)Your deviceTo remember your preferences between sessions.

2. The only network request

If you enter a Pro licence key, the extension sends that key and the product identifier to Gumroad’s public licence API (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify) to check it. Nothing else is included — no page content, no URL, no identifier about you or your browser. The reply is stored locally for 30 days, with a 7-day offline grace period, so the add-on works without a connection.

Gumroad processes that request under its own privacy policy: gumroad.com/privacy. If you never enter a key, the extension makes no network requests at all.

3. Site audits

An audit fetches the addresses you type, from your browser, the way a crawler would. Those websites will see a request from your IP address, without cookies and without a referrer. Nothing about the pages, the results or the report is sent to the developer or to any third party.

4. What is never collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising identifiers.
  • No browsing history, no reading of pages you did not scan.
  • No accounts, names, email addresses or payment details (payments happen on Gumroad, not in the extension).
  • No sale or sharing of any data with third parties.

5. Permissions, and why each exists

  • activeTab — read the current page, only after you click the toolbar button.
  • scripting — return that page’s HTML to the extension for parsing.
  • storage — keep the licence key, settings and the address of the last scan on your device.
  • api.gumroad.com — the licence check described above.
  • optional site access — requested only when you start a site audit, for the sites you entered. If you decline, the one-page scan keeps working.

6. Retention and deletion

Scan results live in your browser’s extension storage and in files you exported. Removing the extension deletes its storage; exported files stay wherever you saved them. To remove the licence from this browser, open the extension and press “Remove” next to your key. To have a licence record deleted at the vendor, contact support with your Gumroad receipt.

7. Children

The extension is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13.
